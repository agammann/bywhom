# ByWhom demo runbook

Prepared **2026-10-08 UTC** for a **target recording under three minutes**. This is the earlier shot and verification plan for the actual extension, not submission copy or suggested narration. The [completed 1:09 video](https://youtu.be/2dNrmiwtEYA) uses clearly labeled fictional local pages instead of the public article sequence below.

## Before recording

1. Use an isolated Chrome/Chromium profile with only the intended demo tabs. Do not expose personal tabs, tokens, account details, or unrelated windows. In `chrome://extensions`, enable Developer mode, choose **Load unpacked**, and select this folder containing `manifest.json`. Pin ByWhom if needed.
2. Open and check the public [WUSF article](https://www.wusf.org/health-news-florida/2026-04-19/your-new-therapist-chatty-leaky-and-hardly-human), [University of South Carolina republication](https://sc.edu/uofsc/posts/2021/01/conversation_artificial_intelligence.php), and [KFF Health News article](https://kffhealthnews.org/mental-health/ai-chatbots-therapy-big-risks-few-regulations/). They worked in the 2026-10-08 test; do not assume their markup or availability is unchanged. Use local fixtures if a publisher page becomes unavailable, and label that substitution honestly.
3. Keep the side panel wide enough to show the three cards and **Show evidence**. Rehearse a keyboard close with Escape and the **Pause animation** toggle. Prepare one short, explicit credit line for the paste demonstration; do not use personal or restricted article text.

## Recording accuracy

- Show the real toolbar click in the isolated profile. Prior browser tests triggered the extension action programmatically; screenshots from the panel smoke are simulations and must not be presented as a human-click recording.
- The extension reads only after the click, processes locally, does not search for an earliest original, and does not bypass publisher restrictions. Visible lines and metadata are claims made by the page, not proof of authorship or ownership.
- If a result differs from the 2026-10-08 snapshot, record the observed result or use a labeled local fixture. Do not force the expected claim or imply ByWhom discovered KFF as WUSF's source.
- The learner chooses and writes any public narration, project description, submission fields, and exit-survey answers. This runbook supplies only mechanics and checkpoints.
